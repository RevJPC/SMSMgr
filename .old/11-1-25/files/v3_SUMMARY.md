# ⚡ v3 Quick Summary

## 🎉 What Changed

### NEW: ➕ Message Anyone
Green "New Message" button lets you text ANY phone number, not just drivers in your list.

### NEW: 📥 Auto-Accept Unknown
Receive messages from anyone. They're automatically added to your contacts.

### IMPROVED: 🧹 Cleaner Settings
Removed duplicate "Test Notification" button.

---

## 🚀 How To Use

### Text Someone New:
```
1. Click "➕ New Message" (green button)
2. Enter phone: +1234567890
3. Enter name: "John Doe" (optional)
4. Click "Start Conversation"
5. Done! Message them.
```

### Receive From Unknown:
```
1. Someone texts your Twilio number
2. Wait 15 seconds
3. They appear in your driver list automatically
4. Under "📱 Ad-Hoc Contacts" team
5. Click and reply!
```
